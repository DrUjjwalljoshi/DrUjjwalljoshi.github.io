MY PERSPECTIVE

Learn from the journey.
Share what helps.

My medical education took place at Kabardino-Balkarian State University (KBSU) in Nalchik, Russia. Experiencing the student side of medical education abroad gave me a practical perspective on the questions students and families need to consider before making an important decision.

Through this website, I aim to share educational content, explain common questions, and help students approach their choices with more context. I also use this platform to bring together my professional profile, educational resources, and the work associated with Doctor’s Inspiration.

This website is being developed as a living resource. Articles, videos, verified updates, and additional professional details can be added as the site grows.

A note on accuracy

Admission rules, licensing requirements, tuition fees, visa policies, and university details can change. Always verify current requirements with the relevant university and official authorities before making decisions.
